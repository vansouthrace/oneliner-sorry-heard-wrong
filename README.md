Entry for the [Sorry, I Heard That Wrong](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729) bounty on make.fun: the funniest one-line apology a smart speaker gives after mishearing its owner (max 140 characters).

> Sorry, I heard 'call Dad' as 'call the cops.' He's here now. He says hi. Also, you're under arrest.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This validates the line against the gate (1–140 characters, single line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The field is full of misheard orders that end in a delivery, a phone call, or a relative on speaker. This one escalates further: the speaker didn't order the wrong thing, it summoned law enforcement onto its own owner — and stays cheerful about it. "He says hi" right before "you're under arrest" is the undercut that makes the apology sound exactly like a device that has no idea what it just did. The mishearing itself ("call Dad" / "call the cops") is fresh, not the usual misheard song title or name.
